Every corner of the internet grows its own small vocabulary, and this one grew a
particularly blunt set. Four words that turn up in more conversations than any
of us would like to admit, each of them doing the job of a full sentence.

They're now a die-cut word series. One word per sticker, cut tight to the
lettering, drawn heavy enough to still read at the size you'd actually use.

## Why words and not more faces

Faces are the fun part. Words are the useful part.

A face is a thing you put on a laptop because you like it. A word is a thing you
put on a laptop because it's *about* something — the flight case that got
dropped, the trade you don't discuss, the pile of stuff in the corner. The word
stickers get used. The faces get admired and left in the packet.

We want both, but we know which one ends up on the road case.

## What's in it

The four above, plus a fifth that keeps getting redrawn and keeps not being
right yet. It'll turn up when it turns up.
