---
title: "Rekt now has a job title"
date: 2026-08-03
summary: "He is Risk, Retroactively. He did not ask for this and he cannot get rid of it."
---

The team page needed roles under the names. Four of them were easy.

Rekt was not easy, because the honest description of what Rekt does is "finds
out". After a fairly short discussion he was given **Risk, Retroactively**,
which is the only job in the building where being wrong first is part of the
process.

He has accepted it with the grace of a man who checked the wallet again this
morning and is now handling anything that involves saying "yeah, that was me" in
public. He has more practice at it than the rest of us combined, and that is a
genuine qualification.
