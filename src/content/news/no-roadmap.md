---
title: "There is no roadmap. There is a list."
date: 2026-09-05
summary: "The difference being that a roadmap has dates on it and a list does not."
---

A roadmap is a promise with a quarter attached, made by people who do not know
what the next quarter contains. We've read a lot of them. Most of them aged
like milk.

So there's a list instead. It lives on the Projects page, everything on it has a
status, and the status is honest — including the ones that say **parked**,
which is the word for "we thought about it and stopped".

Things move up the list when they're ready and get quietly killed when they
aren't. Nothing on it has a date, because we'd only be making the date up, and
you'd only be holding us to it.

When something ships, it turns up here.
