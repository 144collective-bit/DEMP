---
title: "Oracle called the top"
date: 2026-08-25
summary: "With complete accuracy. Nine days after it happened."
---

Oracle came into the room on Monday and announced, with total confidence and no
hedging whatsoever, that the top was in.

He was right. He is always right. The third eye has never once been wrong.

The third eye is also, on current form, running about nine days behind the rest
of the world, which makes it less of an oracle and more of a very ornate
calendar.

He has been asked to write the calls down *before* saying them out loud, and
has agreed on the condition that nobody checks the timestamps.
