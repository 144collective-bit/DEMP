---
title: "No forum. Not now, not later."
date: 2026-08-18
summary: "It was on the list. It is off the list. Here is the reasoning, so nobody has to ask twice."
---

For about a week there was a forum on the plan. Threads, categories, the whole
thing. It has been taken off the plan and it isn't coming back.

The reasoning is not complicated:

- A forum with nobody in it looks worse than no forum.
- A forum with somebody in it needs moderating, every day, forever.
- Neither of those jobs is one this crew wants, and pretending otherwise would
  have produced a ghost town with our name on it inside two months.

There are already places to talk. We're not going to build a worse one and then
slowly stop maintaining it in public.

What you get instead is this page, an RSS feed, and one account to shout at.
That's a smaller promise, and it's one we can actually keep.
