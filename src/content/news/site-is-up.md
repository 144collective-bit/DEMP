There was a version of this where we posted a black square with a date on it,
let it sit for a week, and called that a launch. We got as far as making the
square.

Then somebody pointed out that the site was already finished and the square was
just a way of not shipping it for another seven days.

So: it's up. Seven pages, a crew of five, a sticker range and a place to put
the dispatches. Nothing on it is trying to get you to do anything except look at
some art drawn on black.

If something is broken, it's broken because we built it rather than bought it,
and we'd rather hear about it than not.
